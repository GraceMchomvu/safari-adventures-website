Climbing & Trekking

Ready to trade your flip-flops for hiking boots? Whether you're dreaming of standing on the Roof of Africa at sunrise or exploring misty mountain trails far from the crowds, Tanzania's peaks are calling! From the mighty Mount Kilimanjaro to the wild beauty of Mount Meru, the dramatic slopes of Ol Doinyo Lengai, and the lush green escapes of the Usambara Mountains—we've got treks for thrill-seekers, nature-lovers, and everyone in between.

Most Popular

Mount Kilimanjaro

Conquer Africa's highest peak - 5,895m above sea level. Multiple routes available for different experience levels.

5-Day Marangu Route

Classic "Coca-Cola" route with comfortable hut accommodations

6-Day Machame Route

Scenic route through stunning landscapes including Barranco Wall

8-Day Lemosho Route

Most scenic and best-acclimatized trek through lush rainforests

6-Day Rongai Route

Quiet trails with diverse landscapes and high summit success

6-Day Umbwe Route

Steepest trail designed for experienced climbers

Mount Meru

Perfect warm-up for Kilimanjaro or standalone adventure. 4,566m peak with stunning crater views.

4-Day Mount Meru Trek

Scenic ascent through diverse ecosystems to the summit

Ol Doinyo Lengai

"Mountain of God" - Active volcano with unique natrocarbonatite lava flows.

2-Day Ol Doinyo Lengai

Challenging night climb to witness sunrise from the crater

Usambara Mountains

Lush green mountain ranges perfect for hiking and cultural experiences.

3-Day Usambara Trek

Explore misty trails and traditional villages
