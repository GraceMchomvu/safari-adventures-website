Booking & Cancellation Terms

Please read our terms carefully before making your safari booking with Triple Lions Expeditions

Welcome to Triple Lions Expeditions

We are glad that you are interested in our services. We recommend you read our Booking and Cancellation Terms before sending a booking request. These terms apply to Triple Lions Expeditions and the terms, "we" or "us" should be defined as Triple Lions Expeditions, whereas "you" shall be defined as a client.

Cancellation Terms

If your booking is confirmed and you can't attend the tour, our Cancellation Terms will be applied. For any amendment or cancellation, you must contact our Sales Staff by writing, email, or fax, and shall be effective when received and acknowledged by Triple Lions Expeditions.

When You Cancel

From confirmation up to 61 days before arrival
30% deposit deducted as a booking fee
Between 60 and 45 days before arrival
50% of the invoice amount deducted
Between 44 and 31 days before arrival
60% of the invoice amount deducted
Within 30 days or less before arrival
100% of the invoice amount deducted

No Refund Policy

We are unable to issue any refund in case one or all of the following cases happen:

  • Terminating the trip after its commencement for any reason
  • Missing any travel due to any reason or substituting the booked travel
  • Amending itineraries after their departure

When We Cancel

It is our role to offer services regardless of whatever. But there are cases that we are forced to cancel your booking. We will inform you as soon as possible and the following terms apply:

Our Commitment to You

  • ✓ We will offer an alternative package of similar cost in case your trip was terminated due to other reasons than your failure to make payment or force majeure
  • ✓ If an alternative safari package costs less than the original, we will refund the difference
  • ✓ If you don't agree with the alternative package, we will issue a full refund

Booking Terms

We have booking terms, which upon making a request, you are bound thereto. Triple Lions Expeditions reserves the right to update these terms whenever required. It is recommended to visit our website before booking.

Upon interest in our services, the client should contact the sales team. A Booking Form will be provided, which the client will have to fill out and submit.

Payment Schedule

  • The booking is deemed confirmed if a down payment of 60% of the total cost is received. This percentage varies depending on the total cost of your safari, the type of safari, the size of the group, and the duration of your trip. The remaining balance must be settled at least 60 days before arrival.
  • Full payments are required for chimpanzee trekking safaris to be confirmed. Chimpanzee permits require full payments to be confirmed.
  • For bookings made within 60 days of the date of arrival, a full invoice will have to be paid within 5 days of the date of the Booking.
  • We deduct 30% of the total cost except for Airline expenses. This stake is non-refundable. It covers the costs we incurred when preparing your safari. It is, therefore, not included in the cancellation compensation.

Payment Methods & Important Notes

  • We accept payments wired through our bank account. A bank receipt will be required to confirm the payments.
  • We accept US Dollars only and notes issued before 2006 are a reject.
  • The payments for your Airline tickets should be paid in full before the travel date.
  • We reserve the right to reject bookings at our sole discretion and our bookings are subject to compliance with our terms.

Important Information

Triple Lions Expeditions reserves the right to update these terms whenever required. It is recommended to visit our website before making a booking to review the most current terms and conditions.
