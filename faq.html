Tanzania Safaris

The dry season (June to October) is the best time to visit Tanzania. The wildlife comes together at a few water sources and the trails are accessible. If you are coming to see bird species, November to May is the best time. However, the season is wet, but the scene is picturesque.

Serengeti National Park, Ngorongoro Crater, and Mount Kilimanjaro are the most captivating destinations in Tanzania. Serengeti hosts the Great Migration, the Big Five, and diverse wildlife. The Ngorongoro Crater is the world's largest unbroken caldera and is well-known for its abundance of species. Mount Kilimanjaro is Africa's highest peak. With its height being 5,895 meters above sea level, Kilimanjaro is a challenging peak worth going after.

Kilimanjaro is in northern Tanzania, near the Kenyan border. However, clear views can be experienced from the Kenyan side, especially in the Amboseli National Park.

You should carry with you a valid passport and a visa. Your passport should be valid for at least six months after arrival and should have at least one blank page.

You can obtain a visa for Tanzania either through an online application or upon arrival in the country. Notably, Tanzanian visas are no longer issued by embassies, streamlining the process for travelers. Instead, applicants are encouraged to utilize the electronic visa application system.

You will require:

  • A passport valid for at least six months with at least 1 blank visa page
  • Valid return ticket
  • Evidence of sufficient funds

Upon showing off at the On Arrival Visa facility, you will produce a passport, return ticket, and evidence of sufficient funds to the officers.

An online visa application is highly recommended if your country falls in the Referral Visa category. After filling out the online form and making payments, you will wait for your application to be approved. You will get your visa within ten days.

The packing list varies depending on the type of safari. Warm clothes, travel documents such as a passport, visa, and insurance, any medication, sunglasses, binoculars, and other items will usually be carried.

Zanzibar Safaris

Yes! Zanzibar is a safer option for travel. It has a friendly climate and peaceful political atmosphere. The people of Zanzibar are kind and very receptive to visitors.

Zanzibar is a paradise for those seeking adventure and relaxation. It is home to the world’s exquisite beaches, a perfect setting for relaxation. Zanzibar is also home to significant cultural and historical sites like Stone Town and Prison Island

Yes, if you don’t have yet. A new visa is not required if you already have one from the mainland. Zanzibar is a semi-autonomous state of the United Republic of Tanzania.

Zanzibar is conservative with the majority being Muslims. Avoid revealing clothes, especially when in public places. Wear loose-fitting clothes. Avoid tight-fitting clothes and see-through outfits in public. Wearing shorts and walking shirtless is not the case when you are on the beach or at your accommodation.

Unguja Island, the main island of Zanzibar has the best beaches. Nungwi, Kendwa, Matemwe, Paje, and Jambiani are the most renowned beaches in Unguja.

If you travel to a country where yellow fever is a danger of transmission, you must have a vaccination certificate. Other recommended vaccinations include yellow fever, tetanus, hepatitis, cholera, meningitis, and rabies. We also recommend taking anti-malarial drugs.

The best time is always the dry season (June to October). The sky is clear and there is little rain. Therefore, your visit won’t be interrupted.

Yes, dolphins are common in Zanzibar. There are iconic sites where you can swim with these marine creatures. Menai Bay and Mnemba Atoll are the best diving sites with abundant dolphins.

Swimming is possible in Zanzibar. The beaches are a calming setting to swim. Besides swimming, tourists can enjoy activities like snorkeling, sunbathing, and scuba diving.

Yes! Honeymooners can enjoy a lot in Zanzibar. Whether on a luxurious or budget safari, Zanzibar offers an unforgettable safari experience. It is a perfect romantic gateway to reconcile with your partner after just getting married. Private beaches offer memorable beach excursions.

Yes, Zanzibar is home to reef sharks and whale sharks. The best site to spot sharks is Mafia Island. These sharks are however harmless. You should be comfortable while swimming with these creatures.
